Privacy policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

The short version. Cadence is a planner. Your plan is stored in your browser. If you sign in with an email, a copy is also saved to your account so your other devices can load it. Cadence does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking, and does not sell or rent anything about you. A few features send data to other companies to work, and each one is listed below with exactly what is sent.

Who runs Cadence

Cadence is run by Operator name to be added before launch. For questions about this policy, or requests about your data, use the contact page.

What Cadence stores

In your browser

Everything in your plan is kept in your browser's storage (in the Chrome extension, the extension's own storage):

  • The name you gave Cadence.
  • Your assignments: titles, courses, due dates, instructions, points, and whether they are done.
  • Your activities, their schedules, and your check-ins, including any notes you write on them.
  • Calendar events you add, your school hours, roughly when you sleep, and any one-day changes you make.
  • Your settings, including whether you chose the light or dark theme and whether you have dismissed the notice about storage.
  • If you add them: an Anthropic API key, a Google Calendar access token, and your Canvas calendar feed link. These three are never saved to your account, and leave your device only to reach the service they belong to, with one exception: on the website, your Canvas feed link goes to Cadence's server, which fetches your calendar from Canvas, hands it straight back to your browser, and keeps neither. (A browser does not let a website read another site's calendar itself. The Chrome extension can, so it does not use Cadence's server for this.)

If you use Cadence without an email, this is the only place your plan exists. Clearing your browser's site data, or choosing Delete my data in your account, erases it.

In your account, if you sign in with an email

Signing in with an email is optional. If you do, Cadence stores with its database provider, Supabase:

  • Your email address and the name you chose.
  • A copy of your plan, as described above. The copy leaves out your Anthropic API key, your Google Calendar connection and its events, and your Canvas calendar feed link. Those stay on the device where you set them up.
  • If you use Crew: check-ins for the activities you chose to share, kudos, nudges, and which partners and clubs you are connected to.
  • A count of how many assistant requests your account made today, used only to enforce a daily limit. It is stored against an account ID, not your name or email, and is removed the following day.

Each plan is stored in a row that only its owner can read or change. To sign in, Cadence emails you a code, or a link that does the same thing. That email is sent from a Gmail account that belongs to Cadence, so Google handles it on its way to you. Cadence never has a password for you.

Supabase runs the sign-in, and it keeps its own log of sign-ins: the time, the account, and the IP address and browser the sign-in came from. The person who runs Cadence can see that log.

If you write to us

The contact page has a form. When you send it, your message, the email address you give, and your name if you add one go to Netlify, the company that hosts this site. Along with them Netlify records the IP address and the browser the message was sent from, as it does for every form it receives. Netlify stores the message, checks it for spam with a filtering service called Akismet, and emails it to the person who runs Cadence, so a copy is also in their inbox.

It is used to answer you and for nothing else. It is not added to any list. It is not part of your plan and is not connected to it. Ask for a message to be deleted and it will be, in both places.

What Cadence reads from other services

Canvas

The Chrome extension reads Canvas using the Canvas login you already have in that browser. It reads your name, your courses, and your assignments (title, due date, instructions, rubric, points, submission status, and the items in the assignment's module). This is read by your own browser and saved into your plan. Cadence does not receive your Canvas password, does not create an API token, and cannot see anything in Canvas that you cannot see yourself. If you paste a Canvas calendar feed link, your browser fetches that feed directly.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, Cadence reads events from the calendars you pick so it can plan around them. Those events are kept on your device and are not copied to your account. Only if you separately turn it on, Cadence adds its own planned blocks to a calendar you choose, marks them as Cadence's, and never edits or deletes any event it did not create.

Cadence's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Calendar data is used only to show your calendar and plan around it. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI models. When the assistant uses Claude, events that came from Google Calendar are passed on as a time with no title.

The assistant, and AI

On a copy of Cadence that offers it, every message you send the assistant goes to Gemini, an AI model made by Google, whether you use Cadence with an email or with only a name. If you add your own Anthropic key, it goes to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, instead. Each time you send a message, the following is sent to that company to produce the reply:

  • The message you typed.
  • A summary of your week: the current date and time, your school hours and days off, your sleep times, any one-day changes you have made (such as a shorter evening), your open assignments (title, course, due date, time estimate), your activities with their schedules and how many times you have done each this week, your calendar events for the next seven days (title and time, or time only for events from Google Calendar), the work blocks already planned, and anything that does not fit.
  • Earlier messages in the same conversation.

This goes through Cadence's server, which adds Cadence's key and passes it on to Google. Cadence uses Google's free Gemini API. Under Google's terms for it, Google uses what it receives to improve its products, and people at Google may read it. So do not put anything in a message to the assistant that you would not want a stranger at a company to read: no health details, no passwords, and no private information about other people. If you would rather not send anything to Google, do not use the assistant: everything else in Cadence works without it. Cadence's server does not store your messages or the replies.

If you use Cadence with only a name, your browser first gets a hidden account from Supabase, Cadence's database provider, so that the server can count how many messages it sends in a day. That account is a random ID and nothing else: no name, no email and none of your plan, and the database lets it do nothing but be counted. It is kept in your browser, and removed after 30 days without use.

If a reply from an AI model cannot be had, the assistant reads simple sentences on your own device instead, with nothing sent anywhere. With your own key, it goes straight from your browser to Anthropic under your Anthropic account.

With your own key, Cadence also sends the instructions of an assignment to Anthropic when it writes that assignment's summary, steps and time estimate, and, in the teacher tools, the text of an assignment you dictate.

How Google handles what it receives is described in the Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy, and how Anthropic does in Anthropic's privacy policy.

If you use the microphone button, your browser turns your speech into text. In Chrome, that means the audio is sent to Google to be transcribed. Cadence receives only the text.

Cadence does not share your data with anyone else, and does not use advertising or analytics services.

Cookies and tracking

Cadence does not set cookies and does not track you across sites. It uses your browser's storage to keep your plan, as described above.

A link to Cadence can carry a label saying where it was shared, for example utm_source=newsletter. Cadence removes that label from the address as soon as the page opens.

The label is not recorded or counted.

What other people can see

Nothing, unless you use Crew. Activities are shared unless you switch sharing off for one.

  • An accountability partner you add sees your name and your check-ins for every activity you share: the activity, how long, how it went, and any note you wrote.
  • A club or class you join sees your name, your check-ins for the activities you linked to that club (with the same details), and your progress toward its weekly goal. It does not see check-ins meant for another club, or for no club.

Neither can see your assignments, your calendar, or activities you do not share.

You can end a partnership or leave a club at any time from Crew, and from then on they no longer see your check-ins. A club's leader can also remove you from it.

Deleting your data

  • Without an email account: open your account in Cadence and choose Delete my data, or clear this site's data in your browser.
  • With an email account: open your account in Cadence and choose Delete my data. This erases your account, the saved plan, your check-ins, and your connections to partners and clubs. It cannot be undone. It does not erase entries already in Supabase's log of sign-ins.
  • A message you sent from the contact page is not part of your plan, so neither of those removes it. Ask from the contact page and it is deleted, from Netlify and from the inbox it was sent on to.

You can also ask for a copy of what is stored in your account, or ask for it to be deleted, from the contact page.

Age

Cadence is made for high-school students. It is not meant for children under 13, and children under 13 should not use it or sign in. If you are under 18, check with a parent or guardian before signing in with an email, and follow your school's rules about the tools you use for schoolwork.

Security

Data in your account is protected by database rules: only you can read or change your plan, and your check-ins reach only the partners and clubs described above. Keys and tokens for other services stay on your device. No system is perfectly secure, so avoid putting anything in a note or an event title that you would not want stored.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes. If a change affects what is collected or who receives it, signed-in users will be told in the app.

The rules for using Cadence are in the terms of use.
