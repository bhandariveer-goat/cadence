What happens to your message

It goes to Netlify, the company that hosts this site. Netlify stores it, checks it for spam with a service called Akismet, and emails it to Veer Bhandari, who runs Cadence and answers it. It is not used for anything else, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time. The privacy policy has the detail.
