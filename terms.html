Terms of use

Last updated October 15, 2026

The short version. Cadence is a planning tool. It helps you decide when to do your work; it does not do the work, and it cannot promise you will meet a deadline. Use it honestly, follow your school's rules, and do not try to break it or use it to get at anyone else's data.

Who these terms are between

These terms are an agreement between you and Operator name to be added before launch, who runs Cadence. By using Cadence you agree to them. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to them for you.

What Cadence is

Cadence shows your classes, activities, events and assignments on one calendar, estimates how long assignments take, and plans time for them. It includes an assistant that changes your plan when you describe your day.

Estimates and plans are suggestions. Due dates come from Canvas or from what you type, and either can be wrong or out of date. You are responsible for knowing what is due and when, and for getting it done. Check Canvas or your teacher if something matters.

Your account

  • You can use Cadence with only a name, kept in your browser, or sign in with an email address you control.
  • Do not sign in with someone else's email or use someone else's account.
  • You can stop using Cadence and delete your data at any time from your account screen.
  • Cadence is not for children under 13.

Using Cadence fairly

You agree not to:

  • Use Cadence to break your school's rules, including its rules about academic honesty and about which tools you may use.
  • Try to access another person's plan, account or data.
  • Send the assistant large volumes of automated requests, or use it for anything other than planning your own week.
  • Interfere with the service, probe it for weaknesses without permission, or try to get around its limits.
  • Post anything in Crew (notes, club posts, nudges) that harasses, threatens or demeans another person.

The assistant

The assistant plans your time. It will not write your essays or answer your homework. Its replies come either from rules on your own device or from an AI model, and an AI model can misread a sentence. Every change it makes is listed and can be undone; check the list.

Other services

Cadence works with Canvas, Google Calendar, Google's Gemini and Anthropic's Claude if you connect or use them. Your use of those services is governed by their own terms and by your school's agreement with Canvas. Cadence is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instructure (the maker of Canvas), Google, or Anthropic.

What you put into Cadence stays yours. You give Cadence permission to store and process it only as needed to provide the service to you, as described in the privacy policy.

Nothing in these terms limits any rights you have that cannot legally be limited.
